Jinyu Liu (刘晋煜)

I am a Ph.D. student at Dalian Maritime University, focusing on weak signal sensing with wireless and mmWave radar systems.

My research focuses on weak signal sensing in wireless sensing systems, with applications in contact-free vital signs monitoring, respiratory and cardiac sensing, blood pressure estimation, multi-site physiological sensing, in-vehicle hidden occupant detection, and vital signs monitoring under radar motion.
