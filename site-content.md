## 3. About

### Name

Jinyu Liu (刘晋煜)

### Short Bio

Ph.D. student at Dalian Maritime University working on weak signal and human-centric sensing with mmWave radar.

### Long Bio

My research focuses on weak signal sensing and human-centric sensing using mmWave radar, with applications in contact-free vital signs monitoring, respiratory and cardiac sensing, blood pressure estimation, multi-site physiological sensing, and in-vehicle hidden occupant detection.
